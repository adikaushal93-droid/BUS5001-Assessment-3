Q4 – Google NotebookLM Experiment Log
Date: 16 October 2026

Scenario:
A postgraduate student preparing cloud security revision materials.

Notebook:
BUS5001 Q4 – Cloud Security Study

Source:
UNC5537 Targets Snowflake Customer Instances for Data Theft and Extortion
https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/threat-intelligence/unc5537-snowflake-data-theft-extortion

Test 1 – Summarisation
Requested a 150-word summary with citations.
The response covered stolen credentials, three security weaknesses,
and the distinction between customer compromise and a breach of
Snowflake itself. The response exceeded the requested length.

Test 2 – Cited Question Answering
Asked whether exactly 165 organisations suffered confirmed breaches.
The response distinguished approximately 165 potentially exposed
organisations from a confirmed breach total.
An inspected summary citation supported missing MFA, unrotated
credentials and missing network allowlists.

Test 3 – Mind Map
Generated and expanded the causes, security gaps and mitigation branches.
The map organised key concepts for revision.
Short labels compressed context and required source checking.

Test 4 – Quiz
Attempted two generated questions and inspected explanatory feedback.
The feedback covered stolen credentials, missing MFA and network
allowlists, and rejected an unsupported Active Directory explanation.

Evaluation Limits:
One source and one academic scenario were tested.
Only two quiz questions were inspected.
No formal learning improvement or time saving was measured.
The results do not establish general reliability.
